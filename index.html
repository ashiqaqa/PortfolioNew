About

A designer who also builds the model.

I'm a research-driven Computer Science graduate (MS, gold medalist) specialising in human-computer interaction, deep learning and assistive technologies.

For more than six years I've worked across UI/UX design, graphic design and multimedia production. That mix lets me move between a Figma file and a TensorFlow notebook, and build systems that are both intelligent and accessible.

Right now I'm working on head pose estimation and gesture-based interaction for assistive computing. I'm applying for PhD research in HCI, AI and accessibility for differently abled individuals.

Research

Deep learning

Head pose estimation

I implemented and evaluated seven CNN architectures to predict head orientation from face images, and used data augmentation to improve performance.

  • ResNet50
  • VGG16
  • MobileNetV2
  • EfficientNetB0
  • + 3 more CNNs
Datasets
AFLW2000-3D and 300W-LP
Metrics
MAE, MSE, RMSE and R²
Augmentation
Kornia
Stack
Python, NumPy, TensorFlow/Keras
